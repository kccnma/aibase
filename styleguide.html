Layout Patterns

Stack

The .stack utility creates a vertical flow with consistent spacing between items.

First content item

Second content item

Third content item


<div class="stack">
    <p>First content item</p>
    <p>Second content item</p>
    <p>Third content item</p>
</div>

Cluster

The .cluster utility arranges related items in a row and wraps them when space is limited.

  • Responsive Navigation
  • Grid Patterns
  • CSS Components
  • Documentation

<ul class="cluster">
    <li>Responsive Navigation</li>
    <li>Grid Patterns</li>
    <li>CSS Components</li>
    <li>Documentation</li>
</ul>

Split

The .split utility shares available horizontal space, then wraps its items when the container is narrow.

Primary content

This area shares available space with its sibling.

Supporting content

At narrow widths, this content wraps below the first area.


<div class="split">
    <div>Primary content</div>
    <div>Supporting content</div>
</div>

Sidebar

The .with-sidebar utility keeps a sidebar beside a flexible content area when space allows and stacks them on narrow screens.

Sidebar

Secondary information

Main content

The primary content area grows to fill the available space.


<div class="with-sidebar">
    <aside>Sidebar content</aside>
    <div>Main content</div>
</div>
